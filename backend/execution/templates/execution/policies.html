Incident policies

An incident policy only classifies severity (scale, when escalation is required before closing, who may downgrade). It never decides whether a robot is held: every incident holds its robot. Active = the highest version ever fully approved; if that one is withdrawn there is none. A synthetic demo policy is usable only in demo mode.
