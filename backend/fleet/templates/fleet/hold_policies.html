A new hold binds, for life, to the active policy, else the newest one, else none (the binding reads no setting). Active = the highest version fully approved (engineering and safety for a synthetic policy; engineering, safety and founder for an approved procedure); if that version is withdrawn there is none. In production only an approved procedure releases holds; a hold under anything else needs a two-person re-basing decision. Who must approve an incident hold release is a company decision (B-07); the platform ships no such policy.
