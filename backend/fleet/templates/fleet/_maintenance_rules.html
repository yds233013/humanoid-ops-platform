Maintenance rules

One rule per requirement, in versions. A version is a proposal until the engineering lead and a safety reviewer, two different people and neither of them its author, approve it. The approved version with the highest number applies from its effective time; if that version is withdrawn there is no rule for the requirement (no fallback). Triggers: calendar days, completed visits or active hours, whichever is reached first. The platform never supplies an interval.
